Why the exact time matters

Your Lagna (ascendant) changes roughly every 2 hours. Example: born at 06:42 AM in Jaipur gives a Leo Lagna; born at 08:50 AM the same day gives Virgo, which changes every house in your chart. Use the time on your birth certificate or hospital record.
